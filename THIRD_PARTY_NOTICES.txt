1. CPython `csv.Sniffer`
------------------------

Derived files:
  - rust/datagrunt-core/src/dialect.rs
  - src/datagrunt/core/csv_io/_compute_python.py (the CSV dialect-sniffing
    functions behind `sniff_dialect`)

Source: the `Sniffer` class (`sniff`, `_guess_quote_and_delimiter`,
`_guess_delimiter`) in CPython's `Lib/csv.py`, https://github.com/python/cpython,
including the fixes released in CPython 3.10.22, 3.11.18, 3.13.18 and 3.14.10
(gh-103925, gh-109638, gh-98820).

Summary of changes made (PSF License clause 3):
  - The algorithm is re-implemented in Rust (dialect.rs) and as a standalone
    Python function set (_compute_python.py) instead of being imported from
    the standard library.
  - Regular expressions are written in a form that behaves identically on
    Python 3.10-3.14 and on the Rust fancy-regex engine (no possessive
    quantifiers; CPython's `\w` spelled out as an explicit character class).
  - Results are returned as datagrunt's dialect mapping rather than a
    `csv.Dialect` subclass; an undeterminable sample returns None instead of
    raising `csv.Error`.
  - `has_header` and other `Sniffer` members are not included.
